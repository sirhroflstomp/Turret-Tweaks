# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Turrets turn at your normal mouse sensitivity (no more 0.5 degrees per frame cap)
- Wider aim limits, set separately for left/right and up/down
- Stops you getting stuck against an aim limit
- Narrower field of view and a closer camera while aiming
- Mouse wheel zoom (1x to 4x) on every turret
